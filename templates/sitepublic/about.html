{{ NETWORK_SHORT_NAME }} — the Alliance of NPO Networks — is the united voice of NPO networks working together for a stronger, more equitable South Africa. {{ NETWORK_SHORT_NAME }} brings together NPO networks, associations and organisations to amplify impact, influence policy and build a just and inclusive civil society sector.

Our mission

To strengthen South Africa's civil society organisations by connecting them into a supportive network, helping them build governance and compliance readiness, and enabling them to demonstrate their collective impact.

What we do

  • Connect NPO networks and organisations across South Africa's provinces and sectors.
  • Support organisations to strengthen governance, compliance readiness and operational maturity.
  • Surface funding, training and partnership opportunities.
  • Aggregate and demonstrate the collective reach and impact of the network.
