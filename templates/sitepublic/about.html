About {{ NETWORK_SHORT_NAME }}

{{ NETWORK_SHORT_NAME }} is South Africa's community impact platform, a digital home for NPOs, NGOs, community-based organisations, advice offices, foundations and social enterprises. Organisations join a national community, build a public profile, discover opportunities and partnerships, manage their programmes and community work, and demonstrate the impact they have.

Our mission

To give South Africa's community organisations a single, connected place to be discovered, to run their organisation, and to prove the difference they make, without losing their own records if a specific programme or partnership ends.

What we do

  • Connect organisations to a national community of civil society and to each other.
  • Support organisations to strengthen governance, compliance readiness and operational maturity.
  • Surface funding, training, tenders, events and partnership opportunities.
  • Give every organisation its own workspace to manage programmes, people, finances and evidence.
  • Enable programmes and networks, like sector-specific partner programmes, to work with participating organisations without taking over their data.
  • Help organisations measure and demonstrate their impact to funders, partners and their own boards.
