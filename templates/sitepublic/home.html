About {{ NETWORK_SHORT_NAME }}

A National Umbrella Body for Civil Society

{{ NETWORK_SHORT_NAME }} unites NPO networks across South Africa to strengthen governance, drive compliance readiness and amplify the collective voice of civil society.

  • {% icon "calendar" %}Founded in April 2020 as a national umbrella body for NPO networks.
  • {% icon "users" %}Represents over 78,000 NPOs through its member networks.
  • {% icon "network" %}Founded by seven founding member networks committed to unity and accountability.
  • {% icon "handshake" %}Supported by CIBA as technical partner.
