What is Bohlale Impact

A Digital Home for Community Organisations

Bohlale Impact is a community and operating platform for South African NPOs, NGOs, community-based organisations, advice offices, foundations and social enterprises, built to grow with your organisation, not just list it.

  • {% icon "users" %}Connect: build a public organisation profile and become part of a national community of civil society organisations.
  • {% icon "handshake" %}Collaborate: discover funding, training, tenders, events and partnerships, and apply to join programmes and networks.
  • {% icon "shield-check" %}Manage: run your compliance, governance, programmes, people and finances from one organisation workspace.
  • {% icon "badge-check" %}Measure Impact: capture the work you do and demonstrate it to funders, partners and your own board.
